Quick Answers

Luxury fragrance samples, packaged carefully and explained simply.

How many sprays does each vial contain?

1ML — 18+ sprays · 2ML — 35+ sprays · 5ML — 85+ sprays · 10ML — 170+ sprays · 30ML — 500+ sprays.
Note: 1ML orders arrive in a 2ML vial filled halfway.

Are your fragrances authentic?

Yes — every fragrance is 100% authentic, sourced from trusted authorized retailers or directly from the brand. We never sell clones, fakes, or dupes.

How long does shipping take?

Most orders are processed within 24 hours and delivered within 2–6 business days depending on your location. You'll receive a tracking number as soon as your order ships.

Where can I track my order?

Tracking numbers are sent by email after shipment. Enter your tracking number on USPS.com to follow your order in real time.

Do you offer international shipping?

At this time, we ship within the United States only. We're actively working to expand internationally.

How are the decants packaged?

Each decant is carefully hand-filled and sealed in a premium glass atomizer with a secure cap, then labeled with the fragrance name. Orders ship in padded protective packaging.

Do decants come in the original bottle? What is a decant?

Decants are hand-poured from the original bottle into new glass atomizers — you're receiving the exact same fragrance, simply in a smaller and more affordable size. The original retail bottle is not included.

How do I contact support?

Reach us on TikTok @scentgallery_ or by email. We typically respond within 24 hours.
