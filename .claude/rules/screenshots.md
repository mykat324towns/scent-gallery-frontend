# Screenshot Conventions

## screenshots/LP Inspiration/
Full-page screenshots of landing pages Jaxson wants referenced during website design and rebuilds. When building or redesigning any page, pull from here first to align with the visual direction and patterns Jaxson has curated.

## screenshots/SI Iterate/
Working screenshots captured via Playwright CLI during active website design and iteration. Workflow:
1. Take screenshots as you build and design
2. Analyze what's wrong or improvable
3. Fix, redesign, re-screenshot
4. Repeat until confident the output meets the brief

This folder is a scratchpad — screenshots here are disposable working artifacts, not references.

## screenshots/feature-keep/
Screenshots of sections, features, or concepts from Jaxson's current Scent Gallery website that should carry forward into the rebuild — not copied verbatim, but the underlying idea preserved and reimplemented. When building the new site, review this folder and honor the intent behind each screenshot. Do not replicate exactly; translate the concept into the new design direction.
