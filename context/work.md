# Scent Gallery

**Type:** Ecommerce (DTC)
**Platform:** WordPress / WooCommerce + Elementor
**Product:** Fragrance decants — small sample sizes (1ml–30ml) of luxury colognes, so customers can try before committing to full bottles. Multiple sprayer options, curated product selection.

## Revenue

- Past peak performance: ~$500/day, ~20 orders/day
- Current state: Revenue constrained due to low inventory and limited restocking capital
