## Design Context

### Users
Men 18–30, fragrance enthusiasts, TikTok fragrance community. Shopping on mobile, often post-TikTok. They know decants, comparison-shop on price and trust. They want variety and rotation — not just a single signature scent.

### Brand Personality
Precise, confident, fragrance-culture-native. Three words: **clean, collected, authoritative**. Premium without being pretentious. The brand should feel like a well-lit apothecary — crisp and intentional, not cold. Trust is earned through clarity, not flash.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light mode. Pure white (#FFFFFF) base, #FAFAFA for section alternates. #1C4032 forest green as the primary accent — used for buttons, badges, icons, text callouts, and borders. NOT used for full section backgrounds. Typography: Bodoni Moda (italic, display headlines) + Figtree (body/UI). 7px button radius, 12px card radius. Scroll reveals, no parallax. Shadows are subtle (7–10% opacity black).

**Color system:**
- `--color-bg`: #FFFFFF
- `--color-bg-alt`: #FAFAFA
- `--color-surface`: #F5F5F5 (card backgrounds)
- `--color-on-surface`: #141414 (primary text)
- `--color-on-surface-muted`: #6B6B6B (secondary text)
- `--color-primary`: #1C4032 (forest green — buttons, accents)
- `--color-primary-hover`: #2C6050
- `--color-primary-light`: #1C4032 (green on white — icons, steps, checkmarks)

### Design Principles
1. Clarity over decoration — every element must earn its place
2. Variety is the product — design communicates multi-scent freedom, not just sampling
3. ICP-native — feels like fragrance culture, not a generic DTC store
4. Fast to understand — new user comprehends value prop in under 2.5 seconds
5. Price anchor — $4 entry point should be visible early and often
6. Trust before scroll — authenticity and shipping signals belong at the hero level, not buried below fold
